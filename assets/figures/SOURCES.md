## First-principles bridge review, November 12

`bridge_first_principles.png`/`.svg` are our conceptual schematic, generated by `scripts/draw_bridge_mechanism.py` and informed by Lew et al. (2014), DOI https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.03385 ; Thorp et al. (2011), https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M111.263020 ; and Cai et al. (2016), https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1524292113 . It is not an atomic complex, membrane-spacing estimate or stoichiometric model.

`lipid_binding_structural_evidence.png` contains our PyMOL renderings of deposited coordinates, not reproduced paper figures. Left: bovine prothrombin Gla/lysoPS/calcium, PDB 1NL2, Huang et al. (2003), https://doi.org/10.1038/nsb971 . Right: rat Annexin V/glycerophosphoserine/calcium, PDB 1A8A, Swairjo et al. (1995), https://doi.org/10.1038/nsb1195-968 . The first is a Gla-mechanism surrogate; the second contains a lipid-headgroup analogue without acyl tails. Neither is a neutrophil membrane, a MERTK complex or an Annexin fusion. `scripts/prepare_bridge_review.py` records source-coordinate URLs and hashes; `scripts/build_bridge_review.py` renders the experimental complexes without changing their internal coordinates. The detailed [bridge review](../../docs/first_principles_bridge.md) explains all sources and limitations. `bridge_sources.json` is the coordinate-source snapshot for this review.
