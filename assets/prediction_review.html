Provenance and interpretation

Native mode is a rigid overlay onto an experimental receptor, not a jointly predicted full-receptor complex. Original mode preserves the predicted target and partner geometry and omits unmodeled glycans. All displayed designs remain provisional; no binding, dimerization, efferocytosis or shedding protection is demonstrated.
